# Bonk!

A neon-arcade whack-a-mole game built for mobile — tap the green critters, dodge the purple bombs, beat your best in 30 seconds.

Open `bonk/index.html` in any browser. Single self-contained file: HTML/CSS/Canvas + synthesized WebAudio, no dependencies, no build step.

## Play
- **Tap green critters** for points
- **Avoid purple bombs** — they cost you 3 points and shake the screen
- Rounds are **30 seconds**; bombs get more frequent and critters faster as the clock winds down
- Best score is saved locally on your device
